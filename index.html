Ph.D. Scholar · CSE · IIIT Sri City

Rohit Bantupalli

I study how to keep communication secret after quantum computers arrive. My work spans quantum key distribution, post-quantum cryptography and its hardware implementations, and quantum-walk-based security for connected vehicles (V2X). I also work on observability and self-healing for quantum-secure edge-cloud systems, high-performance simulation of noisy quantum circuits, and DAG-based distributed ledgers.

|research⟩

02

My research, drawn as a circuit: four qubits, one gate per research thread.

q₀H

Quantum Cryptography

Quantum key distribution protocols and how they hold up in real, lossy, multi-party networks, from point-to-point links to large networks.

r ≥ 1 − 2h(Q)(1)

Secret-key fraction for BB84 with one-way post-processing: a key survives only while the error rate Q stays below about 11%.

q₁●

Post-Quantum Cryptography

Lattice-based schemes, and hybrid QKD + PQC designs that stay secure even if one of the two layers is broken.

q₂⊕

Quantum-Secure V2X

Low-latency authentication and key exchange for connected vehicles, with observability and self-healing in edge-cloud systems.

|ψ(t+1)⟩ = S(C ⊗ 𝟙)|ψ(t)⟩(2)

One step of a discrete-time quantum walk (coin C, then shift S), the building block behind QSVeCom.

q₃

Quantum Circuit Simulation

Simulating quantum protocols and noisy circuits, and speeding up simulation with parallel computing.
